It's been a minute since I updated this page. Partly that's because I seem to have come down with a full-blown case of vibe-coding psychosis. I've been working on everything I'm passionate about all at once, with Claude Code and Codex playing genie and granting every wish.

Project Akshara has taken a bit of a backseat, but I plan to get back to it in the coming days and weeks. Meanwhile, here's what I've been actively working on:

- **[This Indian Life](https://thisindianlife.today/)**: sourced charts and plain-language explainers about how India lives.
- **[Measure of India](https://measure.thisindianlife.today/)**: a sister project of This Indian Life, covering India's economy in numbers.
- **[Tipsheet](https://tipsheet.markets/)**: my attempt at building the go-to portal for markets data.
- **[Let Me Fucking Walk](https://letmefuckingwalk.com/)**: a small project I started because I was sick and tired of the lack of proper footpaths in Bangalore.
- **[Soochi](https://soochi.fyi/)**: a simple directory of tools and datasets for people working on public problems, public policy, research and so on.

So yes, lots of public-data work lately. That's what I've been up to.
